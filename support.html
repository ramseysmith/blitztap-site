Gameplay Tips

🎯 Focus on the target first

Before scanning the grid, lock your eyes on the target shape and color. Knowing exactly what you're looking for makes scanning the grid twice as fast.

🔥 Protect your streak

A wrong tap at a 15 streak is devastating. If you're not 100% sure, take an extra half second to confirm. The multiplier gains from a long streak far outweigh the risk of a timeout.

⚡ Watch for near-miss distractors

In Tiers 3 and 4, the grid is full of shapes that share EITHER the right color or the right shape, but not both. Train yourself to verify both attributes before tapping.

🏆 Use the Continue wisely

The rewarded video continue is most valuable when you're close to a tier boundary or deep into a streak. Don't waste it on a score of 5. Save it for when it really counts.
